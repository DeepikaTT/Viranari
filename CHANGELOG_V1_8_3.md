# ViraNari V1.8.3 — Safety Map Scope Fix

## Fixed
- 500 m / 2 km / 5 km now controls anonymous ViraNari presence only.
- Safety-place pins are no longer discarded just because they are outside the selected people radius.
- Hospitals, clinics, police stations, pharmacies, fire stations and verified Safe Nodes are retained up to 15 km.
- Safety Map automatically fits the discovered safety places on first load.
- Added a **Show all safety places** map control.
- Safety summary counts now describe the 15 km safety-place pool rather than the anonymous people radius.

## Preserved
- Live GPS updates feed.
- Victim/responder live tracking and distance/ETA.
- Incoming SOS notification-bar alert and in-app full-screen alert.
- Silent SOS, Safety Compass, Trusted Circle, offline GPS queue, Inbox and History.

Version: `1.8.3+43`.
